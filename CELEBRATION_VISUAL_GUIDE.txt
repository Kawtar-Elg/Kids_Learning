                        🎬 ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
===============================================================================

CONGRATULATIONS ENTRANCE:
─────────────────────────
Type:       Scale + Fade
Start:      scaleX=0.8, scaleY=0.8, alpha=0
End:        scaleX=1.0, scaleY=1.0, alpha=1.0
Duration:   300 milliseconds
Easing:     Default (Linear)
Trigger:    On dialog.show()

COMPLETION ENTRANCE:
────────────────────
Type:       Bounce Scale + Fade
Phase 1:    scaleX=0.5, scaleY=0.5, alpha=0 → scaleX=1.1, scaleY=1.1, alpha=1.0
Duration 1: 300 milliseconds
Phase 2:    scaleX=1.1, scaleY=1.1 → scaleX=1.0, scaleY=1.0 (settle)
Duration 2: 150 milliseconds
Total:      450 milliseconds
Easing:     Default (Linear)
Trigger:    On dialog.show()

LOTTIE ANIMATIONS:
──────────────────
Auto-play:  Enabled
Loop:       Infinite
Speed:      1.0x (normal)
Render:     Hardware accelerated
Format:     JSON (from raw resources)
