# Privacy Policy for Alpha-Pals

**Last Updated: December 23, 2025**

## Introduction

Alpha-Pals ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children using our
educational application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our app.

## Information Collection and Use

### Data We DO NOT Collect

Alpha-Pals is designed with children's privacy as our top priority. We **DO NOT**:

- ❌ Collect any personal information
- ❌ Request names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- ❌ Track user location
- ❌ Use analytics or tracking services
- ❌ Display advertisements
- ❌ Share any data with third parties
- ❌ Require account creation or login
- ❌ Access camera, microphone, or contacts
- ❌ Connect to the internet for data transmission

### Data We Store Locally

The app stores the following data **ONLY on your device**:

- ✅ User progress in learning activities (locally stored)
- ✅ App settings and preferences (locally stored)
- ✅ Drawing history for practice sessions (locally stored)

All data is stored locally using Android Room Database and **never leaves your device**.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Alpha-Pals is designed for children and fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection
Act (COPPA):

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
- No registration or account creation is required
- The app works 100% offline
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No advertisements or in-app purchases
- No links to external websites or social media

## Permissions Used

Our app requests the following permissions:

### Required Permissions:

- **INTERNET**: Only used for loading YouTube videos in the learning section (optional feature). No
  data collection occurs.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Used to check if internet is available before attempting to load videos

### NOT Used Permissions:

We do **NOT** request or use:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Contacts
- Phone
- SMS
- Storage (except for app data)
- Calendar
- Bluetooth

## Third-Party Services

### Lottie Animations

We use Lottie animations (by Airbnb) for visual effects. These are local animation files that do not
collect or transmit any data.

### YouTube Videos (Optional Feature)

The app includes an optional video learning feature that displays YouTube educational content. When
users choose to watch videos:

- Videos are played through YouTube's embedded player
- YouTube's own privacy policy applies to video playback
- No viewing data is collected by our app
- Parents can disable this feature entirely

### ExoPlayer

We use Google's ExoPlayer for video playback. It operates locally and does not collect or transmit
data.

## Data Security

- All app data is stored securely on the user's device
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- App data is automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled
- The app supports Android's backup features (can be disabled by the user)

## Parental Control

Parents have full control over the app:

- No registration required means no personal data exposure
- App can be uninstalled at any time, removing all local data
- Video learning feature can be avoided if preferred
- App works completely offline

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by:

- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Including notice in app updates

## Data Retention and Deletion

- App data is stored only on the device
- Data is automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled
- Users can clear app data through device Settings > Apps > Alpha-Pals > Clear Data

## International Users

Alpha-Pals is available worldwide. All data storage and processing occurs locally on the user's
device, regardless of location.
